5 CRITICAL MISTAKES (01 & 02)

01. CHOOSING MATERIALS BEFORE PLANNING THE LAYOUT

Homeowners frequently fall in love with custom cabinets, exotic stone slabs, or commercial ranges before verifying that those exact dimensions function within the room footprint.

WHY IT MATTERS

Pre-purchased materials force awkward layout compromises and expensive retrofit fees.

CALLOUT: A beautiful material cannot fix a poorly planned kitchen.

02. UNDERESTIMATING THE TRUE PROJECT SCOPE

Focusing solely on visible cosmetic surfaces leads homeowners to ignore the vital behind-the-wall infrastructure required to support modern luxury appliances.

ELECTRICAL

200A Service & Dedicated 50A Range Circuits

VENTILATION

High-CFM Exterior Exhaust Ducting

CALLOUT: A complete plan accounts for the work behind finished surfaces.

5 CRITICAL MISTAKES (03 & 04)

03. DESIGNING FOR TODAY INSTEAD OF HOW YOU LIVE

Designing purely based on an existing layout without analyzing real-world daily behavior leads to friction during meal prep, family gatherings, and morning routines.

CALLOUT: Design around your life—not just the room.

04. SACRIFICING FUNCTION FOR LOOKS

A visually stunning kitchen that fails ergonomically quickly becomes a source of daily frustration. High-end design must serve human utility first.

CALLOUT: The best kitchen looks beautiful and works beautifully.

5 CRITICAL MISTAKES (MISTAKE 05)

05. FAILING TO PLAN FOR THE UNEXPECTED

Every home hides history behind its walls. Assuming zero surprises during demolition creates unnecessary financial friction when hidden site conditions appear.

CALLOUT: The goal isn't to expect problems. It's to be prepared if they appear.

CONTRACTOR VETTING & SELECTION PAGE 12 / 14

Hire with absolute confidence...

10 QUESTIONS TO ASK BEFORE HIRING A CONTRACTOR

1. Who manages my project day-to-day?

Ensure a dedicated manager serves as single contact.

2. What is in the written proposal?

Verify line items cover demolition, labor & permits.

3. How are change orders handled?

Require written authorization before out-of-scope work.

4. Who pulls municipal permits?

Reputable contractors handle all filings themselves.

5. How do you protect my home?

Ask about containment barriers and dust protection.

6. How do you handle site surprises?

Confirm photo documentation & cost-option reviews.

7. What is the project schedule?

Look for phase schedules accounting for lead times.

8. How are lead times managed?

Ensure custom items are ordered before demolition.

9. What is the final walkthrough?

Confirm formal punch-list process before final payment.

10. Can I see completed references?

Review past client work of similar scope.
